# SpendWise – Project Statement

## Problem Statement

College students often have difficulty tracking their daily expenses and managing their monthly budgets. Small expenses such as food, transportation, shopping, and entertainment can accumulate without proper tracking.

SpendWise aims to provide a simple web-based solution that allows students to record, organize, and analyze their expenses while monitoring their monthly budget.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on personal expense management for college students. Users can record their expenses, categorize them, set monthly budgets, monitor their spending, and view spending analytics through a dashboard.

The system is intended for individual use and focuses on tracking personal expenses rather than managing banking or financial transactions.

## Target Users

- College students
- University students
- Students living in hostels or away from home
- Students who want to monitor their personal spending

## High-Level Features

- User registration and login
- Add, edit, and delete expenses
- Categorize expenses
- Set monthly budgets
- Track total and remaining budget
- Search and filter expenses
- View spending analytics
- View monthly spending summaries
